About the System

Built for Students, Powered by Science

DietSync is a student project developed to demonstrate how technology can simplify clinical nutrition management. It connects users, dietitians, and administrators in a unified digital platform.

✓ BMI-based calorie target calculation
✓ Real-time food logging with instant calorie feedback
✓ Role-based access for Users, Dietitians & Admins
✓ Clean daily reports with progress tracking
✓ Dietitian-created meal plans per user

How It Works

Three Steps to Better Nutrition

1

Create Your Profile

Enter your body metrics — age, weight, height, and activity level — to generate your calorie target.

2

Log Your Meals

Record what you eat throughout the day. The system calculates calories and compares against your goal.

3

Get Your Plan
